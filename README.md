# Block Hoops

**Play:** https://muhammadalims.github.io/block-hoops/

1v1 clay streetball in the browser, built with Three.js in a single HTML file (same clay engine as Galli Cricket).

## Courts
- **NYC Park Cage**: chain-link cage between brownstones. Winners ball. Ball over the cage: turnover.
- **Manila Barangay**: tight court, tin roofs, a jeepney. Hit the jeepney: turnover. Bank shots off the sari-sari store's tin overhang count.
- **Venice Beach**: sunset court by the ocean. Sea breeze bends 2-pointers (watch the flag). Winner stays.

Everywhere: first to 11, 1 inside the arc and 2 from behind it, make it take it, check at the top of the key, and take it back past the arc after a defensive rebound or steal.

## Controls
- **1v1:** WASD/arrows move · hold Space to jump, release at the top (green band) · close to the rim Space lays it in or dunks · E crossover (can break ankles) · on defense Space blocks, E steals (risk a foul).
- **2 players:** P1 WASD + Space + E · P2 Arrows + Enter + /.
- **Phone (landscape):** left stick moves, SHOOT (hold/release), CROSS/STEAL.
- C camera · T time of day · Esc pause.

## Development
Edit `game.html`, then run `./build.sh` to regenerate `index.html`.
